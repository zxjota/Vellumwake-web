Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Vellumwake (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the “App”). By downloading, accessing, or using Vellumwake, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

We collect limited data to provide and improve the App experience. The categories of information we may collect include:

  • Information you provide directly: If you contact us via email or submit feedback, we may retain your email address and the content of your message to respond to your inquiry.

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the App and its features;

  • Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities to enhance user experience;
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent security incidents or abusive behavior.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR & other)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or other jurisdictions with similar data protection laws, we rely on the following legal bases to process your information:

  • Legitimate interest: To maintain and improve our services, ensure security, and communicate with you when necessary.
  • Consent: Where you have explicitly provided information (e.g., sending us an email), we process it based on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following limited circumstances:

We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.
